# Contributions sign off (DCO), no CLA, no retroactive sign-offs

Contributions require a `Signed-off-by:` trailer (Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1) — the
contribution mechanism a GPL project can operate without lawyers. A CLA was rejected because the
project has no legal entity to hold the assignment and no license-dualization plan that would
justify asking; contributors keep their copyright, and the license is already fixed for anyone who
forks. Commits predating adoption keep no sign-off and nothing is rewritten retroactively; the
policy applies from its adoption forward and to release-tagged work first. `CONTRIBUTING.md` and a
commit-hook or CI check land with the M1 milestone rather than delaying this decision.
